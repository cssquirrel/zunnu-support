### Privacy Policy for Zunnu

**Effective Date:** 2026-09-19 

This Privacy Policy explains how Zunnu ("the App") collects, uses, and safeguards information when you add the App to your Discord server or interact with it. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. 

### 1. Information We Collect

To function properly, the App automatically interacts with certain data provided by the Discord API. This may include: 

- **User Data:** Discord User IDs, usernames, and avatars.
- **Server Data:** Server IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, and server configurations.
- **Message Content:** The App only processes message content when explicitly triggered by a command or interaction (e.g., slash commands or prefixes). We do not log or store your regular chat history.

### 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data strictly to provide and improve the App's functionality. This includes: 

- Executing commands and features requested by users.
- Saving custom server settings (e.g., prefix choices, moderation logs, custom roles).
- Maintaining stability and debugging errors.
